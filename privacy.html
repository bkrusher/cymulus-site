Privacy Policy

Cymulus period tracker for iPhone · Last updated 7 November 2026

The short version: Cymulus has no servers, no accounts on our side, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your cycle data stays on your iPhone. The maker of Cymulus never receives it, so it is never sold, shared, or available to hand over to anyone.

What Cymulus stores, and where

  • Your logs and settings (period days, flow, discharge, pain, mood, symptoms, test results, temperatures, notes, reminders and preferences) are saved in a file on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection.
  • Widgets read a small summary (for example days until your next period and your current phase) through an App Group that only Cymulus and its widgets can open, on the same iPhone.
  • Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Optional encrypted iCloud backup

iCloud backup is off until you sign in with Apple or turn it on in Settings › Cloud backup. From then on it backs up automatically a few seconds after each change, and encrypts your data on your iPhone with AES-256-GCM before uploading it to Cymulus’s private folder in your own iCloud account. The key is a random 256-bit key kept in your iCloud Keychain (which Apple end-to-end encrypts), or, if you choose, a key derived from a passphrase only you know. Neither Apple nor the maker of Cymulus can read the backup. You can delete the backup at any time in Settings › Cloud backup.

Optional Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, Cymulus keeps your Apple user ID, and the name and email Apple shares, on your iPhone only, to help find your backup on a new iPhone. They are never uploaded, included in exports, or backed up. Sign out in Settings › Account, and stop using Sign in with Apple for Cymulus in iPhone Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple.

Exports

You can export your data as a file and share it however you like. The readable export is not encrypted, so anyone with the file can read it. Encrypted backup files can only be opened with your key or passphrase.

What Cymulus does not do

  • No third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • No data is collected by the developer, as described in Cymulus’s App Store privacy label (“Data Not Collected”).
  • Cymulus does not read from or write to Apple Health.
  • Cymulus makes no network requests of its own. It only talks to Apple’s iCloud when you turn on iCloud backup, and to Apple when you use Sign in with Apple.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete everything removes all logs and settings from your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same. Settings › Cloud backup › Turn off and delete cloud copy removes the iCloud backup and its key.

Children

Cymulus is not directed at children under 13.

Health information

Cymulus’s predictions are estimates, not medical advice, and Cymulus is not a method of birth control. See the in-app Health notice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and in the app, with a new date at the top.
