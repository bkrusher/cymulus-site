Help for Cymulus, the private period tracker for iPhone.

Contact

Email arainave@gmail.com. Please don’t send your cycle data; describe the problem and your iPhone and iOS version.

How accurate are the predictions?

Cymulus uses a weighted average of your recent cycles, with a range that widens when your cycles vary. A positive ovulation (LH) test or a temperature rise this cycle moves your next period estimate toward ovulation plus your luteal phase. Predictions sharpen as you log more cycles. They are estimates, not medical advice, and not a method of birth control.

Adding widgets

Open Cymulus once. Then touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, and search for Cymulus.

Reminders are not arriving

In Cymulus, open Settings › Reminders and tap Allow notifications. Then check iPhone Settings › Notifications › Cymulus.

Moving to a new iPhone

Before you switch, turn on Settings › Cloud backup in Cymulus. On the new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple ID with iCloud Keychain on, open Cymulus and tap Restore my data from iCloud. You can also export an encrypted backup file and restore it from Settings › Your data.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete everything erases all logs and settings on your iPhone. If you use iCloud backup, also tap Turn off and delete cloud copy.

Privacy

Cymulus has no servers and collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
